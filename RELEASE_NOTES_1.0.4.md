## Installation and signatures

Save your work and close After Effects and AI clients using AfterWIP before upgrading. Use the installer manually, then reopen After Effects and the AI client. Existing account/device data is kept outside the installer payload; no account or entitlement reset is performed.

Both packages have verified Adobe CEP publisher/timestamp signatures and SHA-256 integrity. The update manifests use the existing RSA signing key and sequence 35. Windows has no Authenticode signature; macOS has no Apple Developer ID signature/notarization. SmartScreen or Gatekeeper may show warnings. Do not disable operating-system protections.

The owner explicitly instructed publication after being informed of those missing OS signatures. Required native build, package, config and Adobe/RSA verification completed. Manual installation, Adobe workflows and account acceptance are left to the owner; they are not claimed as tested. Earlier releases remain available.
